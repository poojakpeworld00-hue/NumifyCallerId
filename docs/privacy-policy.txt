PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: [EFFECTIVE_DATE]

This policy explains what the Contacts: Caller ID & Block app ("the app",
package com.callerid.numberlookup.home) collects, why, who it is shared with,
and how to have it deleted. The app is published by [COMPANY] ("we", "us").

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the app.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE SHORT VERSION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- The app needs your contacts and call log to name the people who call you.
- Your contacts are uploaded to our server once, so that callers can be
  identified. This is described in full in section 3.
- The app does not record phone calls. It cannot: Android has not permitted
  third-party call recording since Android 10.
- You can ask us to delete your data at any time, from inside the app, at
  Settings -> Data Deletion.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. INFORMATION HELD ONLY ON YOUR DEVICE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The following is read on your phone, used there, and not sent to us:

CALL LOG (READ_CALL_LOG)
  Numbers, dates, durations and call types from your phone's call log. Used to
  build the Recents list, the call details screen, the post-call screen, and the
  Ask AI answers about your own calling patterns. Ask AI works out its answers on
  your device from this data.

PHONE STATE (READ_PHONE_STATE)
  Tells the app when a call starts and ends, so the caller ID card can be shown
  while the phone rings and taken away when it stops.

BLOCK LIST
  The numbers you choose to block, stored on your device.

YOUR SETTINGS
  Theme, language, and which features you have turned on.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. INFORMATION SENT TO OUR SERVERS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

There are exactly two things the app sends to us. Both go to
contact-saver.dailymorningupdate.com over HTTPS.

3.1 YOUR CONTACTS - UPLOADED ONCE

  What:   The name and phone number of each contact stored on your phone.
  When:   Once per installation, the first time you grant contacts permission.
          It is not repeated, and it does not run in test builds of the app.
  Why:    Caller identification works by matching an unknown number against
          number-and-name pairs contributed by the people who use the app. Your
          contacts contribute to that, and it is what allows the app to name a
          caller who is not in your own phone book.
  Format: A CSV file containing those two fields, and nothing else. No email
          addresses, photographs, notes, groups, or account information are
          included.

  If you are not comfortable with this, do not grant the contacts permission.
  The app will still show your call log, block numbers and run its tools; it
  will not be able to name people from your phone book.

  You can have this removed. See section 8.

3.2 NUMBER LOOKUPS

  What:   A single phone number, sent when the app needs to identify it.
  When:   When an unknown number calls you, when a call from an unknown number
          ends, and when you search a number on the Lookup screen.
  Why:    To return the name, region, carrier and spam status held for that
          number.
  Note:   Numbers already saved in your phone book are named from your phone and
          are not sent for lookup.

5. PERMISSIONS, AND WHY EACH IS ASKED FOR
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Each is requested when the feature that needs it is first used, and the rest of
the app keeps working if you decline.

  Contacts (READ_CONTACTS)       Name the people already in your phone book, and
                                 the one-time upload described in section 3.1.
  Call log (READ_CALL_LOG)       Build Recents, call details and the post-call
                                 screen.
  Phone (READ_PHONE_STATE)       Detect when a call starts and ends.
  Make calls (CALL_PHONE)        Place a call when you tap a call button.
  Display over other apps        Show the caller ID card while a call is
  (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)          ringing, over whatever else is on screen.
  Notifications                  Caller alerts, including on the lock screen.
  (POST_NOTIFICATIONS,
   USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT)
  Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO)      Voice search in Contacts, and the sound-level
                                 meter in Tools. THE APP DOES NOT RECORD CALLS
                                 AND CANNOT DO SO.
  Internet, network state        Number lookups, ads, configuration.
  Foreground service             Keep the caller ID card on screen for the
                                 duration of a call.
  Vibrate                        Haptic feedback.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. WHAT WE DO NOT DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- We do not record, listen to, or transcribe your calls.
- We do not read your SMS messages. The app does not request SMS permissions.
- We do not collect your precise location. The app does not request location
  permissions. Advertising partners may infer approximate location from your IP
  address.
- We do not sell your personal information.
- The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
  information from them.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. HOW LONG WE KEEP IT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Uploaded contact records are kept while they remain useful for caller
identification, or until you ask us to delete them. Lookup requests are retained
only as long as needed to operate and protect the service. Data held by the
third parties in section 4 is kept under their own retention policies.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. YOUR CHOICES AND YOUR RIGHTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

DELETE YOUR DATA
  In the app: Settings -> Data Deletion. This submits a request to remove the
  data we hold about you. The contacts and call history stored on your phone
  itself are not affected.
  By email: write to [SUPPORT_EMAIL] from the address you wish to identify, or
  tell us the phone number concerned.

WITHDRAW PERMISSIONS
  Android Settings -> Apps -> Contacts: Caller ID & Block -> Permissions. You can
  revoke any permission at any time.

ADVERTISING
  Android Settings -> Privacy -> Ads lets you reset or delete your advertising
  ID. Buying Premium removes advertising from the app.

CORRECT A WRONG NAME
  If a number is identified incorrectly, write to [SUPPORT_EMAIL] and we will
  correct it.

Depending on where you live you may also have the right to access, correct,
export or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to complain to your
data protection authority. Write to [SUPPORT_EMAIL] to exercise any of these.

Our service providers, including those in section 4, may process data in

NOTES FOR THE DATA SAFETY FORM  (not part of the published policy)

Declare these as COLLECTED and SHARED/TRANSFERRED off the device:

  Contacts        - name and phone number, uploaded once (section 3.1).
                    Purpose: app functionality. NOT optional if the user grants
                    contacts permission, so declare it as required for the
                    feature rather than optional.
  Phone number    - each number looked up (section 3.2). Purpose: app
                    functionality.
  Device or other Advertising ID - AdMob and Meta Audience Network.
                    Purpose: advertising.
  App activity / diagnostics - Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics.

Declare as COLLECTED but NOT shared:

  Call log        - read on device for Recents, details and Ask AI. It is not
                    transmitted. Play still requires it to be declared.

Do NOT declare:

  Audio / call recordings - the app does not record anything. RECORD_AUDIO is
                    used by voice search and the sound-level meter only.
  Precise location - not requested.
  SMS - not requested.

Also required for this app:

  - A Permissions Declaration for READ_CALL_LOG. Caller ID is an approved use,
    but it must be declared or the release is blocked.
  - The contacts upload in section 3.1 is the single most scrutinised thing in
    this listing. It is disclosed here in full and in plain words on purpose:
    an undisclosed contact upload is a removal, not a warning.
