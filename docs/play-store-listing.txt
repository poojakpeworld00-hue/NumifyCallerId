FULL DESCRIPTION  (3958 / 4000)
==============================================================================

Contacts is a caller ID, spam blocker and dialer in one — so you know who is
calling before you pick up, and the numbers you never want to hear from stop
ringing.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CALLER ID THAT WORKS WHILE THE PHONE RINGS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• A caller ID card appears over the incoming call with the name and region we
  have on file for that number
• Unknown numbers are looked up as the call comes in, not after you have missed it
• A summary after each call, so you can see who it was at a glance

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SPAM AND FRAUD BLOCKING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Suspected spam, scam and telemarketing numbers are flagged as they call
• Block any number in one tap, straight from the call log or the caller ID card
• A block list you control — add, review and remove numbers whenever you like
• Blocked calls stop ringing instead of interrupting you

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
NUMBER LOOKUP
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Search any phone number and see the name, region and carrier details we have
• Check a missed call before you decide whether to ring back
• Works for numbers that are not in your contacts

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A DIALER BUILT FOR ONE HAND
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Type a number and get the actions you actually want — add to contacts, send a
  message, look it up, open WhatsApp or ask AI about it
• Recent calls grouped by day, filtered by all, incoming, outgoing or missed
• Call details for every interaction with that number

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CONTACTS, TIDIED UP
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Search your contacts by name, phone number or email address
• Voice search when typing is awkward
• Favourites at the top, and a filter for which account a contact came from
• See every account on the phone in one place

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ASK AI ABOUT A CALLER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Ask questions about your calls in your own words and get a straight answer
• A verdict on an unknown caller, drawn from your own call history
• Suggested replies when you cannot pick up
• Answers are worked out on your phone from your call log, contacts and block list

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WHY THE APP ASKS FOR PERMISSIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Phone and call log — to show who is calling and to build your recent calls list
• Contacts — to name the people already in your phone book
• Display over other apps — to show the caller ID card while a call is ringing
• Microphone — used only by voice search and the sound-level meter. This app does
  not record calls
• Notifications — for caller alerts when the screen is locked

Each permission is asked for when its feature is first used, and the rest of the
app keeps working if you decline.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
YOUR DATA
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Caller identification draws on publicly available and community-contributed number
information, so not every number can be identified. You can request deletion of the

details are in our Privacy Policy, linked on this page.

Spotted a number identified incorrectly? Contact us from this listing and we will
put it right.
