What changed, and why

1 Hero header absorbs the search bar

The header, protection card and search pill are three separate stacked blocks today, each with its own margin. Merging them into one gradient hero and floating the search pill over its bottom edge reclaims roughly 40dp of vertical space.

Net effect: one more Recent-activity row is visible above the fold on a 360×640 device.

2 Protection card becomes a live stat strip

“Protection on · scam calls auto-blocked” is static text — it says the same thing on day 1 and day 100, so users stop reading it.

Three counters (Blocked / Identified / Accuracy) give the screen something that changes daily, which is the strongest retention lever a caller-ID home screen has. The original line survives underneath as a secondary row with a Manage affordance.

3 Quick actions get their intended colours

DashboardFragment.bindQuick() already passes a tint and a container colour per action, but the two lines that apply them are commented out at DashboardFragment.kt:349–351 — so all four tiles render identical primary-violet.

The redesign uncomments them: Dialer violet, Lookup purple, Blocklist red, Tools green. Colour becomes a scanning shortcut instead of decoration.

4 Ad slot is labelled, not raw

Today the native ad sits between two full-bleed hairlines on a hard #ccc background — it reads as a broken image in dark mode, where #ccc is brighter than anything else on screen.

A “Sponsored” label plus a surface-coloured card keeps the required visual separation, satisfies ad-labelling policy, and stops the slot from being the loudest element on a dark screen.

5 Recent rows become cards with an action

Flat rows leave the right edge dead. Adding a trailing call-back button (block-back on spam rows) turns the most common follow-up into one tap instead of row → detail → call.

Spam rows carry a faint danger-tinted border so the list is scannable without reading a single word.

6 Dark mode is real, not inherited

Every surface here resolves through values-night/colors.xml. The gradient hero keeps the brand violet in both themes — matching how the splash already behaves — while cards drop to #141824 and borders to #2A3145.

Nothing was removed. The +91 country selector, the permission-hint row (rowPermHint, hidden here since it only appears when permissions are pending), the call-log permission empty state (rowRecentPermission), the See all link, the native ad slot, the bottom nav and the AdMob banner all keep their existing IDs and behaviour — this is a layout and token change, not a feature change.
