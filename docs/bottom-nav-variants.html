Bottom navigation — 10 variants

All ten show the app's current five destinations: Recents · Contacts · Blocklist · Tools · Lookup. The bar is a custom LinearLayout of cell_nav includes, not a BottomNavigationView, so every one of these is reachable by editing cell_nav.xml plus the selection code in MainShellActivity.select().

Reading these against your actual constraint

Five tabs at 360dp is the real problem. That's 72dp per tab. "Blocklist" needs ~46dp at 8.5sp bold, so the labelled variants (1, 4, 5, 6, 10) work but leave almost no breathing room — and on a 320dp device they will truncate. Variants that hide inactive labels (2, 3, 8) or all labels (9) are the ones that genuinely fit five.

Variant 7 solves it differently — promoting Lookup to a centre FAB drops the bar to four items, which is the count the layout was designed around. It also matches how the app already treats lookup: the "Who's calling?" card on Recents is the primary entry, so the FAB reinforces rather than duplicates it.

Cheapest to build: 1 (already shipped), then 4 and 10 — both are a single extra View in cell_nav.xml toggled in select(). Most work: 7, which needs the FAB lifted out of the bar and the banner re-anchored beneath it, and 2/3/8, which animate a width change per tab so they need a ValueAnimator on layout_weight to avoid snapping.

Badges are drawn on Blocklist in variant 9, but they drop into any of these — the natural counts you already have are blocked-today and missed calls.
